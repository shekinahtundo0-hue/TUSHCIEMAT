# MASTER PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
## NEXT-GENERATION SCHOOL MANAGEMENT, DIGITAL LEARNING & EDUCATION ECOSYSTEM

### 1. YOUR ROLE

Act as a world-class team consisting of:
- Senior EdTech product architect
- Senior full-stack software engineer
- UI/UX designer
- Database architect
- Education technology specialist
- Cybersecurity engineer
- Data analytics engineer
- AI integration specialist

Your task is to design and develop a complete, production-oriented, next-generation educational technology platform for the Kenyan education market, with an architecture capable of expanding throughout Africa.

This is an independent product. Do not copy Zeraki's branding, interface, source code, proprietary content or intellectual property. Use established education technology products as functional benchmarks while developing a distinct and more comprehensive experience.

The objective is to create a unified ecosystem that connects school administrators, teachers, parents, students, private tutors and educational institutions.

The platform must be visually exceptional, technically robust, responsive, secure and genuinely functional.

---

# 2. PRODUCT VISION

Build one integrated platform with six interconnected systems:

1. School Management System.
2. Academic Analytics and Examination Management.
3. School Financial Management.
4. Digital Learning Management System.
5. Tutor Discovery and Booking Marketplace.
6. AI-Powered Academic Intelligence System.

All six systems must communicate through a unified database and permission-controlled infrastructure.

The platform should eliminate fragmented school operations by bringing academic administration, financial management, communication, digital learning and student development into one environment.

### Target users

- School owners and directors.
- School administrators and principals.
- Teachers and academic departments.
- School accountants and bursars.
- Parents and guardians.
- Primary and secondary school students.
- Private tutors and learning institutions.
- Platform super administrators.

Support CBC/CBE-aligned education structures in Kenya, while allowing configurable international curricula and examination systems.

# 16. NON-NEGOTIABLE DEVELOPMENT RULES

1. Build real functionality, not decorative interfaces.
2. Never use fake statistics presented as actual school data.
3. Every dashboard must use real database records.
4. Every form must perform its intended operation.
5. Every button must have a working action.
6. Every table must support the required operations.
7. All user roles must have distinct experiences.
8. Enforce permissions on the backend and database.
9. Never compromise existing functionality when adding features.
10. Never silently replace working integrations.
11. Provide database migrations for schema changes.
12. Test all important workflows.
13. Provide useful error messages.
14. Ensure that financial records remain consistent.
15. Do not expose credentials or sensitive student information.
16. Avoid unnecessary duplication of components.
17. Maintain clean, reusable and documented code.
18. Do not claim a feature is complete until it has been implemented and tested.

If an integration requires external credentials, clearly identify the missing configuration instead of simulating a successful connection.

The finished product must feel like a serious, premium education technology company serving thousands of schools and millions of learners.

Its distinguishing advantage should be the seamless connection between:

SCHOOL OPERATIONS + ACADEMIC INTELLIGENCE + FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT + DIGITAL LEARNING + PRIVATE TUTORING + AI-POWERED PERSONALISATION.

The three main experiences must remain clearly differentiated:

ADMINISTRATORS manage the institution.

PARENTS monitor and support their children's education.

STUDENTS learn, practise, improve and achieve.

Begin by inspecting the existing project, if one exists. Present the proposed architecture, database relationships, role-permission matrix and implementation plan before making major structural changes.

**The objective is not to produce a website that looks like an education platform. The objective is to build an education platform that schools can actually operate on every day.**
